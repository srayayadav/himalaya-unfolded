8. **3 thumbnails** per long video (1280×720, 3–5 big words, high contrast, clearly different: place-led, person-led, big-question). Use thumbnail A first; B/C for YouTube's Test & Compare.
9. **Upload text** per video (section 7), plus one honest share post per long video for Santosh to post in Nepali diaspora Facebook groups or r/Nepal (max 1–2 posts a week).
10. **Send to Santosh**: titles + files + upload text, and ask: "approve, or what to change?" Change what he asks for. **Nothing is published without his OK.**

## 5. Realistic style guide (new from 9 Nov)

- **Images:** photorealistic, "cinematic still from a historical documentary, 35mm film, natural light, muted warm sepia grade, shallow depth of field". 16:9. No text in images.
- **People:** historical or generic people only, stylised by costume and era. **Never** the likeness of a real living person, and no real photos of real people. For named historical figures, use a respectful, non-portrait depiction (costume, from behind, at a distance) and always put an on-screen name label.
- **Same character across shots:** reuse one fixed description (e.g. "a Nepali Gurkha rifleman of the early 1900s, brown skin, short black hair, lean weathered face, wide-brimmed khaki slouch hat pinned up on one side, khaki wool tunic, leather belt") and a reference image if your tool supports it.
- **Check accuracy:** khukuri blades bend **down/forward** at an "elbow", with the sharp edge on the inner curve (Santosh checked this against a real photo). Gurkha slouch hats, era-correct uniforms, Nepali architecture (no Chinese pagodas or castles), no modern objects in history scenes.
- **Motion:** slow zoom/pan on every still; 10–20 moving shots per video (walking, wind, mist, water, flags). Free option: Wan 2.2 or Stable Video Diffusion on Kaggle's free GPU (account "epasal").
- **Maps:** real borders (Natural Earth), simple and clean, with gold labels.
- **Captions:** big bold yellow (#FFE14D), black outline, 2–3 words at a time, bottom centre.
- **Transitions:** soft fades plus a warm light-flash at big moments. Light film grain and vignette.
- **Sound:** narration at about -14 LUFS, soft ambient/music bed (royalty-free only, e.g. YouTube Audio Library), a soft whoosh or singing bowl at cuts.
- **Disclosure:** realistic AI images and video of historical scenes → in YouTube Studio set **"Altered or synthetic content: Yes"**. Description line: "Narration uses an AI voice. Visuals are AI-generated reconstructions."
