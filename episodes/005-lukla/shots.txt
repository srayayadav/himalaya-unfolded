GLOBAL MOTION / EDITING RULES
- Generate 10–20 genuine moving clips where the chosen free image-to-video workflow permits; otherwise use licensed real footage for motion-heavy scenes.
- Avoid fake cockpit POV, fabricated ATC dialogue, crash reenactment, inaccurate runway markings, impossible aircraft movement, and AI-generated map borders.
- Use gentle 35mm documentary grade, muted warm highlights, light grain, restrained vignette.
- Captions: bold yellow #FFE14D, black outline, 2–3 words at a time, bottom centre.
- Sound mix: narration target around -14 LUFS integrated; keep music and effects below narration.
- Transitions: soft fades; reserve warm light-flash for major chapter changes.
