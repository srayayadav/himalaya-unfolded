TITLE
Why Lukla Is the World's Most Dangerous Airport

ALTERNATE TITLE (safer wording if you want to avoid the absolute superlative)
Why Lukla Is One of the World's Most Challenging Airports

DESCRIPTION
A runway just 527 metres long. A steep uphill slope. High-altitude air, fast-changing mountain weather and terrain that can limit a go-around.

Discover why Tenzing-Hillary Airport at Lukla is one of the most challenging places for mountain aviation — and why this small airport is a lifeline for Nepal's Khumbu region.
